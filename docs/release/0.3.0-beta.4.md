# Open Manuscript Studio 0.3.0-beta.4

Date: 2026-10-07

## Research modules

This beta adds nine discipline-focused research modules:

- History and Archives
- Religious Texts
- Critical Text Edition
- Corpus Linguistics
- Musicology
- Cultural Heritage
- Social Research Methods
- Legal Sources
- Research Reproducibility

The modules add focused, documented workflows for research tasks inside Studio.
Source discovery remains bounded to the configured scholarly portals and providers
available to the installation.
